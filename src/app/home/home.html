Vanjeevi Ramayya – The Green Crusader of Telangana

Who He Is

Vanjeevi Ramayya is a renowned Indian environmentalist, widely known as the “Green Crusader of Telangana.” Born in 1935 in Telangana, India, he is a former soldier who later dedicated his entire life to tree plantation and environmental protection. He spent decades traveling from village to village, inspiring communities to protect nature and promote greenery.

His Contribution

Over his lifetime, Ramayya planted and helped protect millions of trees across Telangana and neighboring regions. He motivated students, farmers, government officials, and villagers to actively participate in afforestation initiatives.

His efforts focused on roadside plantations, restoration of degraded lands, and conducting environmental awareness campaigns to instill responsibility toward nature in society.

Awards & Recognition

In recognition of his exceptional contribution to environmental conservation, Vanjeevi Ramayya was honored with the Padma Shri Award in 2017, one of India’s highest civilian honors. He was also recognized by multiple environmental organizations and state governments for his lifelong service to nature.

Why He Is Important

Vanjeevi Ramayya proved that a single individual can create a massive and lasting environmental impact. His work inspired numerous green movements and eco-initiatives across India.

He remains a powerful role model for initiatives related to tree registration, compensatory plantation, and community-driven environmental action.
